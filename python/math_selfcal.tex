\par Our residuals are this:
\begin{equation}
S = \sum_{p\neq q} \sum_{mn} d_{pq}^{mn} - g_p^{ma} M^{ab} \overline{g_q^{bn}}.
\end{equation}
$p$ and $q$ are baselines, $m$ and $n$ are correlations ($\in {r,l}$). Repeated indices are summed. 
What we minimize is the norm of $S$, that is, $S\overline{S}$, or $|S|^2$.

\par As ``selfcalibration'', we use the solves Jones matrices and the data used to solve the Jones matrices, to solve for model. 
We do this to see if there is an model differences. 
It will also indicate the performance of our fitting solution.
We do this by evaluating the gradient and using Gradient descent. 
The same way we solved for Jones matrices.
